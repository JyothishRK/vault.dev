----
1. Mesh
    - No of cables: n * (n - 1) / 2
    - No of ports: (n - 1) per device
    - Reliability: Highest
    - Cost: Highest (based on cable)
    - Security: Yes
    - Disadv : High maintenance
    - Point to point comms - dedicated communication.
2. Star
    - No of cables: n
    - No of paths: 1 per device
    - Reliability: Single point of failure at hub
    - Cost: Normal
    - Security: No
    - Point to point comms.
3. Bus
    - No of cables: n + 1
    - No of paths: 1 per device
    - Reliability: Single point of failure backbone wire
    - Cost: Normal (more than star)
    - Security: No
    - Multipoint network - high chances of collision
4. Ring
    - No of cables: n + 1
    - No of paths: 1 per device
    - Reliability: Single point of failure backbone wire
    - Cost: Normal (more than star)
    - Security: No
5. Hybrid ^SdSNew8b
